In short: this game requires no account. The developer does not collect, store or sell any of your personal data. Your progress stays on your own device. The only party that sees any device information is Google AdMob, which serves the ads.

1. What the developer collects

Nothing. The game requires no sign-up and never asks for your name, email, phone number or any identifying information. It contains no analytics or tracking software. No data reaches the developer's servers — in fact, this game has no server.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally in the app's own storage and never leaves your device:

  • Game progress: coins, unlocked balls and skins, achievements, records
  • Settings: language, music and sound-effect toggles and volume
  • The local record of what you have purchased

Deleting the app erases all of it. Note that the game has no cloud backup, so this cannot be undone.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)
The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. When you choose to watch one, Google may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device and OS information, and your interactions with the ad, in order to deliver and measure it. This data is processed by Google; the developer cannot access individual user data.
See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Apple (in-app purchases)
All paid items are handled by Apple's in-app purchase system. Your payment method and billing details never pass through the developer, who only learns whether a given purchase succeeded.

4. App Tracking Transparency

As required by Apple, the game asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier. You may decline. Ads still work and still pay out their rewards if you do — they simply will not be personalised to your interests. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

5. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, please get in touch and it will be dealt with immediately.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You remain in control through:

  • Deleting the app, which erases all local data
  • Turning off tracking permission, or resetting your advertising identifier, in iOS Settings

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page, with the effective date at the top updated to match.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to yenbowie2@gmail.com.
